Sunny Squeeze Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Sunny Squeeze ("the game") is published by Evgeny Shkadov. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any account. Your game progress (cash, stands, upgrades, missions and settings) is stored only on your device. From Settings you can create a backup code that contains your progress; it is shown to you and goes only where you choose to send it.

Gameplay analytics

To understand how the game is played and improve it, the game may send a small number of anonymous gameplay events (for example that a day was finished, a stand was opened or a purchase was completed) together with a random identifier generated by the game. These events contain no name, contact details, location or device identifiers, and are not used for advertising.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device model and ad interactions. On iOS you are asked whether to allow tracking; if you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. In regions that require it, you are also asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

Purchases such as Remove Ads, gem packs and the Sunshine Club subscription are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Purchase status is managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase receipts so the game can unlock what you bought and restore it on a new device. Learn more at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Subscriptions

The Sunshine Club renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions. Purchases are governed by Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can turn off ad tracking at any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. Deleting the game removes all progress stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
