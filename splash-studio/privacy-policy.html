Splash Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Splash Studio ("the game") is published by Evgeny Shkadov. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any account. Your game progress (levels, coins, boosters, gallery) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: short ad breaks between levels and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device model and ad interactions. On iOS you are asked whether to allow tracking; if you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. In regions that require it, you are also asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

Purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Kit, Rescue Kit, coin packs) and the optional Studio Club subscription are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Purchase status is managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts so the game can unlock what you bought and restore it on a new device. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Studio Club renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can turn off ad tracking at any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. Buying Remove Ads or joining Studio Club removes ad breaks. Deleting the game removes all progress stored on your device.
